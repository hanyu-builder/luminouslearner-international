Smoothing Physics

$$V_{out}(t) = V_{peak} \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{RC}}$$

When the AC voltage drops below the capacitor's stored voltage, the diodes reverse-bias (turn red) and block current from returning to the source. The capacitor acts as a temporary battery, discharging exponentially through the load resistor $R$ to maintain the voltage until the next AC cycle pumps it back up.
