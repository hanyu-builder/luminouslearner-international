Because human hearing spans an incredibly vast range of physical intensity (a trillion-fold difference between a whisper and a jet engine), we use a logarithmic scale.

A 10 dB increase represents a 10-fold increase in physical intensity ($W/m^2$), but to human ears, it simply sounds roughly "twice as loud".

Note: As shown in the chart above, perceived loudness depends on frequency. A 60 dB sound at 100Hz sounds much quieter than a 60 dB sound at 1,000Hz. True perceived loudness is measured in Phons.
