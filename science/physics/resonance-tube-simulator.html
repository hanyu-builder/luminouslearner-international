Nodes vs Antinodes

Sound is a longitudinal wave. An Open End allows air molecules to move freely, creating a Displacement Antinode (max movement). A Closed End blocks movement, creating a Displacement Node (zero movement). Resonance occurs when these boundary conditions perfectly fit the tube length.
