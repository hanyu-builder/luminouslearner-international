How to Apply It

Step 1: Point your Right Thumb in the direction of the conventional current ($I$).

Step 2: The natural curl of your Fingers dictates the circular path and direction of the Magnetic Field lines ($B$).

(Alternative grip for coils)

Step 1: Curl your Fingers around the coil in the direction of the circulating current ($I$).

Step 2: Your Right Thumb now points in the direction of the Magnetic Field ($B$) inside the core (towards the North pole).
