Quantum Theory of Light

Light acts as discrete packets of energy called photons. A photon can eject an electron from a metal only if its energy (E = hf) exceeds the metal's binding energy, known as the Work Function (Φ).

$$K_{max} = hf - \Phi$$
$hf$: Photon Energy $\Phi$: Work Function
