Observation

In the Rutherford Model, nearly all mass and positive charge is concentrated in a tiny nucleus. Force is proportional to \( 1/r^2 \). Particles hitting dead center experience massive repulsion, scattering backwards!

In the Plum Pudding Model, the atom is a neutral cloud. By Gauss's Law, the net force outside is zero. Inside, the diffuse positive charge provides a negligible force. Particles pass straight through.
