Stochastic Modeling of Radioactive Decay

\( N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t} \)
\( t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\lambda} \)

Simulation Setup

Each die represents a radioactive nucleus. The roll represents a unit of time. Because decay is a stochastic (random) process, we cannot predict which nucleus will decay, only the macroscopic probability over time.
