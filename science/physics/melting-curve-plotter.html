Core Concepts to Observe:

  • Constant Temperature: During melting, T stops rising. Thermal energy breaks intermolecular bonds (Latent Heat) instead of increasing kinetic energy.
  • Slope differences: The slope ΔT/Δt = P/mc. Notice the liquid line is less steep than the solid line? That's because water has a higher specific heat capacity (4186 J/kg°C) than ice (2100 J/kg°C)!
