1

Isothermal Expansion

Gas expands at constant hot temperature ($T_h$). Heat ($Q_h$) flows in from reservoir to do work. $P \propto 1/V$.

2

Adiabatic Expansion

Reservoir removed (insulated). Gas continues expanding, using its internal energy to do work. Temp drops to $T_c$. $P \propto 1/V^\gamma$.

3

Isothermal Compression

Gas is compressed at constant cold temp ($T_c$). Work is done on the gas, pushing waste heat ($Q_c$) into the sink.

4

Adiabatic Compression

Insulated again. Gas is compressed further, forcing its internal energy and Temperature back up to $T_h$, completing the cycle.
