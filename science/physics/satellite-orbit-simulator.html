Physics Explained

vc = √(GM / r)

To achieve a perfect Circular Orbit, the tangential velocity must be exactly enough so that the object "falls" at the exact curvature of the Earth.

ve = √(2GM / r)

Escape Velocity is reached when Total Specific Energy $\ge 0$. The object has enough kinetic energy to overcome the gravitational potential energy well and will never return.
