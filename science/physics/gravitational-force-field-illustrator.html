Physics Principles

$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$

Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation states that the attractive force ($F$) is directly proportional to the product of masses, and inversely proportional to the square of the distance ($r^2$).

$\vec{g}_{net} = \sum \vec{g}_i$

Superposition Principle: The net gravitational field at any point in space is simply the vector sum of the individual fields from every mass. Hover the probe to see these component vectors!
