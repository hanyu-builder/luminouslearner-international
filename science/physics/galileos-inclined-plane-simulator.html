Galileo's Insight

Before calculus and accurate stopwatches, freefall was too fast to measure. Galileo used inclined planes to "slow down" gravity.

s = ½ a t²

Because s is directly proportional to t², graphing them yields a perfect straight line, proving visually that acceleration is uniform.
