Dispersion Explained

Why does White Light split?

Because the diffraction angle θ depends on wavelength λ, different colors bend by different amounts. Red light (longer λ) diffracts at a wider angle than violet light (shorter λ), creating a spectrum at n = 1, 2, ...

The Central Maximum (n = 0)

At n = 0, θ = 0° for all wavelengths. There is zero path difference, so all colors recombine to form a bright, pure white spot perfectly in the center.
