Ionizing Power vs. Penetrating Power

Penetration \(\propto\) \(\frac{1}{\text{Ionizing Power}}\)

Radiation Source

Alpha (\(\alpha\)): Heavy, slow, and highly ionizing. Stopped completely by a thin sheet of paper or skin.

Beta (\(\beta\)): Fast-moving electrons. Medium ionizing power. Stopped by a few millimeters of aluminum.

Gamma (\(\gamma\)): High-energy electromagnetic waves. Weakly ionizing but highly penetrating. Attenuated (reduced) by thick lead.

Mixed Source: Emits all three types of radiation simultaneously, allowing direct comparison of penetrating powers.
