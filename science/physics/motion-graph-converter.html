Calculus Connections

Differentiation (Slopes)

Slope of the s-t curve gives velocity.
Slope of the v-t curve gives acceleration.

Integration (Signed Areas)

The signed area under the v-t curve determines the change in displacement. Area below the x-axis means the particle moves backward!

Derivative: a(t) = dv/dt
Integral: s(t) = ∫ v(t) dt
