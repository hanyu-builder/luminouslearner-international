The Lorentz Force

F = q(v × B)

The magnetic force is always perpendicular to both velocity ($v$) and the magnetic field ($B$).

qv⊥B = m(v⊥²/r)

Because the force is perpendicular to motion, it does zero work and cannot change the particle's speed, only its direction. It acts perfectly as a Centripetal Force, forcing the particle into a circular orbit.
