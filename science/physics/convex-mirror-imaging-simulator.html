Why Convex? Notice that no matter where you place the object, the image is always formed between the Pole and Focus. This creates a scaled-down image, giving convex mirrors a significantly wider field of view than flat mirrors.

The Paraxial Approximation: The mathematical reflections happen at the flat vertical plane ($x=0$), not exactly on the curved arc. Real spherical mirrors suffer from spherical aberration. To make the math simple ($1/f = 1/u + 1/v$), we assume all rays are close to the principal axis!
