Core Physics Concepts:

  • Ideal Gases: Have negligible intermolecular forces (PE ≈ 0), so Internal Energy is purely Kinetic (U ≈ KE). Try turning Bond Strength to 0.
  • Sandbox vs. Reality: In reality, adding heat (Temperature) eventually breaks bonds during a phase change, which causes Potential Energy to increase towards 0 (become less negative). In this sandbox, you control them independently to study how they sum together!
