Wave Phenomena

Reflection & Parabola

Waves bounce off walls. In a Parabolic Reflector, incoming plane waves reflect and converge perfectly at a single focal point.

Refraction

Waves travel slower in Shallow Zones. When a wavefront hits shallow water at an angle, the side that enters first slows down, causing the entire wave to bend (Snell's Law).

Diffraction & Slits

A plane wave passing through a Single Slit spreads out spherically (Huygens' Principle). Two slits create two overlapping spherical waves.

Interference & Doppler

Interference: Two synchronized sources create an observable pattern of constructive (high) and destructive (flat) nodes.

Doppler: A moving source compresses waves ahead of it (shorter $\lambda$) and stretches them behind (longer $\lambda$).
