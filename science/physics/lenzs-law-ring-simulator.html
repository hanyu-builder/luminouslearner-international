Concept Guide

As the magnet falls, its magnetic field intersects the conductive tube. This changing magnetic flux (\(\Delta\Phi\)) induces an electromotive force (e.m.f, \(\varepsilon\)).

According to Lenz's Law, the induced eddy currents create their own magnetic field that opposes the motion of the magnet, slowing its fall.
