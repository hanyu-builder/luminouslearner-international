The Paraxial Approximation: Notice how the mathematical ray reflections in the simulator happen precisely at the flat vertical plane ($x=0$), not exactly on the curved arc of the mirror? Real spherical mirrors suffer from spherical aberration, where rays hitting the outer edges don't focus perfectly. To make the math simple ($1/f = 1/u + 1/v$), we assume all rays are very close to the principal axis!
