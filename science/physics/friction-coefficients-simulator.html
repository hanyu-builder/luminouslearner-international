Understanding the Graph

Static friction perfectly matches your "Net Push" tendency (Applied Force + Gravity Down Ramp) to keep the block at rest, up to a breaking threshold ($F_{s,max}$).

Once the net push exceeds this threshold, the block slides and friction drops to a constant Kinetic value ($F_k$), allowing acceleration.
