Kepler's Third Law

The square of a planet's orbital period ($T^2$) is directly proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis of its orbit ($a^3$).

Law of Harmonies

$$T^2 = \left( \frac{4\pi^2}{GM} \right) a^3$$
Using Earth Units ($T$ in Years, $a$ in AU), the constant simplifies to 1, leaving exactly $T^2 = a^3$.
