Kinetic Model

Evaporation occurs when liquid molecules gain enough Kinetic Energy (KE) to break intermolecular bonds at the surface.

Because the highest-energy molecules escape, the average KE of the remaining liquid drops. This causes evaporative cooling, pulling heat from the surroundings.
