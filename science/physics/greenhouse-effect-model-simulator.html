The Physics Model

1. Albedo (Reflection)

Short-wave light hitting ice/clouds can bounce back to space without being absorbed or changing wavelength.

2. Short-wave Transparency

The Sun is very hot (~5800 K), emitting short-wave radiation. Greenhouse gases are largely transparent to these wavelengths.

3. Wavelength Shift

λmax ∝ 1/T (Wien's Law)

The cooler Earth (~288 K) absorbs solar energy and re-radiates it as long-wave infrared (IR) radiation.

4. Absorption & Scattering

GHG molecules vibrate at frequencies matching long-wave IR. They absorb the IR photon, and re-emit it in a random direction. Downward re-emission traps heat!
