Newton's First Law

"An object at rest remains at rest, and an object in motion remains in motion at constant speed and in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced force."

If Fnet = 0 (Deep Space)

No acceleration ($a = 0$). Velocity is perfectly constant. The strobe dots will be equally spaced forever.

If Fnet ≠ 0 (Friction)

Friction acts as an unbalanced force opposing motion, causing negative acceleration. The strobe dots get closer together until it stops.

Passenger Analysis

  • Smart Sam
    Holding the strap. The strap applies an unbalanced external force, allowing him to decelerate safely with the bus.
  • Careless Carl
    Not holding on. When the bus brakes, no force acts on Carl. His inertia keeps him moving forward relative to the ground until the windshield provides an unbalanced stopping force!
Reference Frames When the bus brakes, a force is applied to the bus, but not to loose objects inside. Relative to the camera (moving with the bus), Carl seems to accelerate forward magically. In reality, Carl is maintaining his state of motion while the bus slows down beneath him!
