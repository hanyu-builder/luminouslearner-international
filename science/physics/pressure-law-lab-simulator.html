Core Physics Concepts

  • The Dual Effect: Pressure increases for TWO reasons when heated: Particles hit the walls more frequently AND harder (greater momentum transfer per collision).
  • Absolute Zero (0 K): Notice the graph extrapolates to 0 kPa at 0 K. At absolute zero, all translational kinetic energy ceases, resulting in zero collisions and zero pressure.
