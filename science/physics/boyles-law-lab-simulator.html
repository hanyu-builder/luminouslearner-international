Core Physics Concepts:

  • Isothermal Assumption: Boyle's Law assumes Temperature is constant. In reality, compressing gas quickly heats it up (Adiabatic). We assume the piston moves slowly enough for heat to escape, keeping kinetic energy constant.
  • Kinetic Theory: Pressure increases not because particles move faster, but because in a smaller volume, they collide with the walls more frequently!
