Process Guide

  • 1. Photolysis: PSII splits H₂O into O₂, H⁺, and e⁻.
  • 2. ETC: Electrons pump H⁺ into the lumen, building a gradient.
  • 3. Energy Carriers: PSI creates NADPH; ATP Synthase creates ATP.
  • 4. Carbon Fixation: The Calvin Cycle consumes CO₂, ATP, and NADPH to build Glucose!
