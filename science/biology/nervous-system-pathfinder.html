Spinal Reflex Arc vs. Voluntary Conscious Pathway

High-yield neurobiology comparison for IB, AP Biology, A-Level & Grade 12 exams.

Feature ⚡ Spinal Withdrawal Reflex (Involuntary) 🧠 Voluntary Action (Conscious)
Integrating Center Spinal Cord Gray Matter (via Relay / Interneuron); bypasses brain for motor initiation. Cerebral Cortex (Somatosensory Cortex → Association → Primary Motor Cortex).
Circuit Distance & Synapses Short path; only 2 CNS chemical synapses (Dorsal Horn + Ventral Horn) plus NMJ. Long ascending/descending tracts + many cortical synapses (each adds ~0.5–1.5 ms synaptic delay).
Typical Reaction Time ~35 – 50 ms (Rapid survival protection) ~200 – 280 ms (Flexible & adaptable decision)
When Is Pain Felt? AFTER muscle withdrawal! Parallel ascending tract reaches Somatosensory Cortex ~75 ms later. BEFORE motor command is sent down the Corticospinal Tract.
Effect of MS Demyelination Loss of Schwann/Oligodendrocyte myelin abolishes saltatory jumping → leaked ions slow reaction to ~150+ ms. Both peripheral nerves and long CNS white-matter tracts slow severely (~650+ ms) or suffer conduction block.
