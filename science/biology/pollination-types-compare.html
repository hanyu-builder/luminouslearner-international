Abiotic (Wind)

Pollen Grain
Petals Small, green, or entirely absent. No need to attract pollinators.
Scent/Nectar Absent. Energy is conserved for pollen production instead.
Pollen Grains Smooth, light, aerodynamic. Produced in massive quantities to compensate for wind randomness.
Anthers Dangling outside the flower on long filaments to catch wind gusts.
Stigma Large and feathery/net-like, hanging outside to catch drifting pollen.

Biotic (Insect)

Pollen Grain
Petals Large, brightly colored. Contains "nectar guides" to direct insects.
Scent/Nectar Strong scents and sugary nectar act as an attractant and reward.
Pollen Grains Sticky and spiky/rough. Designed to cling to animal fur or exoskeletons.
Anthers Enclosed within flower. Placed strategically to rub against visiting insects.
Stigma Sticky, flat pad enclosed within flower. Catches pollen brushed off the insect.
