Student Misconceptions

1. The "Half-Chromosome" Myth: Students think haploid cells only have "half" an X. Telophase I cells are haploid but have fully replicated 'X' shaped chromosomes.

2. The "Double Marker" Myth: Students think crossing over gives a gamete BOTH markers. A chromosome only has ONE locus for that gene, so it can only carry ONE marker color!
