Tunica Media (Muscular Artery)

Thickest concentric layer in arteries, composed of circularly arranged smooth muscle cells and elastic sheets. Stretches during ventricular systole and recoils during diastole (Windkessel effect) to maintain continuous blood flow.

Exam Key: Thick smooth muscle enables vasoconstriction & vasodilation to regulate systemic blood pressure and redirect blood to active organs.
