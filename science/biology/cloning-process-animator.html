Somatic Cell Nuclear Transfer (SCNT)

Phase:
1. Setup

State Machine Output

Preparation (The 3 Actors)

To clone an organism, we need:
1. An unfertilized Egg Cell from an Egg Donor.
2. A Somatic (Body) Cell from the subject to be cloned.
3. A Surrogate Mother to carry the embryo.

Interactive Sequence

Extract and discard the Haploid (n) nucleus from the egg cell to create a blank biological factory.

Inject the Diploid (2n) somatic nucleus. The egg now contains the full genetic code of the clone subject!

A mild shock tricks the 2n egg into acting fertilized. It begins to undergo cleavage (cellular division).

The resulting Blastocyst is implanted into the uterus to develop into a full genetic clone.
