🧬 Antibody Matcher

Opsonization & Phagocytosis Lab

Antibodies use a Lock and Key mechanism. The Variable Regions (Paratopes) on the two arms of the Y-shape must physically match the geometry of the pathogen's Epitope.

Binding does not kill the pathogen directly. It Opsonizes (tags) it, signaling a Macrophage to come and consume it!

How to Play

  • 🖱️ Drag an antibody towards the pathogen.
  • AD
    Rotate the antibody while dragging to align the arms.
  • ⚙️ Mouse Wheel also rotates held objects.
