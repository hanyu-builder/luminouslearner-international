Negative Pressure Breathing & Boyle's Law

Pulmonary ventilation is driven by Boyle's Law (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂). When the diaphragm contracts (moves down), thoracic Volume increases, causing internal Pressure to drop. Air flows IN to equalize the pressure.
