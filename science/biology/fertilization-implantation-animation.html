Day & Stage Anatomical Location Cells & Ploidy Stem Cell Potency Zona Pellucida & Key Biological Mechanism
Day 0.0–0.4: Fertilization Ampulla of Fallopian Tube n + n → 2n Gametes → Zygote Acrosomal enzymes digest path through Zona; Cortical Reaction hardens Zona to block polyspermy.
Day 0.5–0.9: Zygote (Syngamy) Ampulla 1 cell (2n = 46) Totipotent Intact (~120 µm). Male & female pronuclei fuse chromosomes; 2nd polar body extruded after Meiosis II completes.
Days 1–3: Cleavage (2 → 4 → 8) Fallopian Tube (Cilia + Peristalsis) 2 → 4 → 8 cells (2n) Totipotent Intact (Constant total volume!): Mitosis without cell growth shrinks blastomere size; prevents ectopic tubal implantation!
Day 4: Compacted Morula Uterotubal Junction → Uterus 16 – 32 cells (2n) Totipotent → Lineage Split Intact but thinning. Tight junctions compact solid mulberry ball; outer vs. inner cell polarity established.
Day 5: Early Blastocyst Uterine Cavity ~70–100 cells (2n) ICM = Pluripotent Osmotic fluid creates Blastocoel; differentiates outer Trophoblast (placenta) & Inner Cell Mass (embryo).
Day 6: Zona Hatching Uterine Cavity ~120+ cells (2n) ICM = Pluripotent Breached & Shed! Proteases digest hole in Zona; blastocyst squeezes out so sticky trophoblast can touch endometrium.
Days 7–9: Implantation & hCG Maternal Endometrial Wall Bilaminar Disc (2n) Pluripotent → Multipotent Syncytiotrophoblast invades endometrium & secretes hCG → rescues ovarian Corpus Luteum to sustain Progesterone!
