🔗 Breaking the Chain

Epidemiological SIR Model

Infection spreads through physical contact. We can halt an epidemic by breaking specific links in the Chain of Infection:

  • Susceptible: Vulnerable to the pathogen.
  • Infected: Actively shedding virus.
  • Recovered/Vax: Immune human shields.
