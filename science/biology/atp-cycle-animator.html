ATP Cycle

Biological Energy Transfer

ATP

Adenosine Triphosphate

Chemical Equation

ATP + H₂O ⇌ ADP + Pi + Energy

The Misconception: Breaking bonds doesn't release energy; it requires energy!

The Reality: ATP is like a compressed spring. The negative phosphate groups repel each other. When Water (H₂O) comes in and splits the unstable terminal bond (Hydrolysis), forming new bonds with water releases a massive amount of energy compared to the tiny amount required to break the bond.

Energy Released for Cellular Work!
