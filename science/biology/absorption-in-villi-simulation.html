1. Nutrient Monomer Transport & Vascular Destination

Digested Nutrient Apical Entry (Brush Border) Inside Enterocyte & Exit Target Vessel & Destination
Glucose & Galactose Na⁺ Co-Transport (Secondary Active Transport) via SGLT1 + Facilitated Diffusion. Requires mitochondrial ATP for basolateral Na⁺/K⁺ pump; exits via GLUT2. Blood Capillaries → Hepatic Portal Vein → Liver
L-Amino Acids & Dipeptides Na⁺ / H⁺-Coupled Active Transport & Facilitated Diffusion carriers. ATP-dependent gradient maintenance; facilitated diffusion across basolateral membrane. Blood Capillaries → Hepatic Portal Vein → Liver
Fatty Acids & Monoglycerides Simple Diffusion directly through phospholipid bilayer (non-polar, zero ATP!). Re-esterified into triglycerides in Smooth ER → packaged by Golgi into Chylomicrons → Exocytosis. Central Lacteal (Chyle) → Thoracic Duct → Subclavian Vein
Water (H₂O) Osmosis via Aquaporin channels & tight junctions. Follows osmotic water potential gradient created by active absorption of Na⁺, glucose & amino acids. Blood Capillaries → Hepatic Portal Vein

2. Structural Adaptations of Intestinal Villi (Fick’s Law of Diffusion)

Massive Surface Area (~600×)

Long coiled ileum + circular folds + millions of finger-like villi + microscopic brush-border microvilli maximize contact area.

1-Cell-Thick Epithelium

Single layer of columnar enterocytes (~25 µm) minimizes diffusion distance between lumen chyme and underlying capillaries/lacteal.

Steep Concentration Gradient

Dense capillary blood flow and rhythmic villus smooth muscle swaying continuously carry absorbed nutrients away.

Abundant Mitochondria

Enterocytes are packed with mitochondria performing aerobic respiration to supply ATP for active transport & exocytosis.

Wide-Pored Central Lacteal

Overlapping lymphatic endothelial flaps admit large (~200 nm) lipoprotein chylomicrons that cannot enter blood capillaries.

Brush-Border Enzymes & Goblet Mucus

Immobilized maltase/peptidases finish digestion right at carrier channels, while goblet mucus protects the delicate 1-cell lining.
