Student Misconceptions

1. The "Instant Fold": Proteins don't snap into 3D shapes instantly. Exposed hydrophobic regions will bind to *other* proteins first, causing useless aggregations!

2. The "Magnet": In this game you drag the protein. In reality, chaperones don't "suck up" proteins. They bump into them randomly via Brownian Motion!

The Mechanism

1. Hydrophobic walls catch the unfolded protein.

2. 7 ATP bind & Cap attaches. Interior shifts to Hydrophilic!

3. The Timer: ATP slowly hydrolyzes to ADP. This gives the protein ~10s to undergo "hydrophobic collapse" inside the safe room.

4. Not Always Perfect: Sometimes a protein fails to fold on the first try and must re-enter the cycle!
