Major Pollutants Released

  • ▪
    SO₂ (Sulphur Dioxide)

    Byproduct of burning sulfur-containing fossil fuels (like coal).

    Health Effect: Constricts airways, triggers asthma attacks, and inflames respiratory linings.

    Eco Effect: Main precursor to Acid Rain, destroying aquatic habitats.

  • ▪
    PM10 & PM2.5 (Particulate Matter)

    Suspended microscopic soot, ash, and heavy metals from smokestacks.

    Health Effect: PM2.5 crosses the blood-gas barrier, causing cardiovascular disease, arrhythmias, and lung cancer.

    Eco Effect: Settles on leaves, blocking sunlight and reducing photosynthesis.

  • ▪
    NOx & VOCs (O₃ Precursors)

    Nitrogen Oxides and Volatile Organic Compounds react in sunlight to form Ground-Level Ozone (O₃).

    Health Effect: Ozone acts like a chemical sunburn on lung tissue, severely reducing lung capacity.

    Eco Effect: Causes necrosis in plant leaves, stunting crop and forest growth.

Health & Ecological Impact

Physiological Danger Summary
  • Respiratory: SO₂ and O₃ cause severe airway inflammation and COPD.
  • Cardiovascular: PM2.5 enters the blood, causing systemic toxicity, plaque buildup, and heart attacks.
Ecological Danger Summary
  • Acid Rain: Lowers soil and water pH, killing fish and leaching toxic aluminum.
  • Botanical Necrosis: Ozone and acid physically burn leaf tissue, halting photosynthesis.
