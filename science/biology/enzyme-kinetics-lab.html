Vmax: Max reaction velocity when enzymes are saturated. It drops if enzymes are inhibited (Non-Comp) or denatured (extreme Temp/pH).
Km: Substrate concentration [S] required to reach 1/2 Vmax. Indicates binding affinity (high Km = low affinity).
