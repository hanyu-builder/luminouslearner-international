Biological Role ♀ Female Structure & Function ♂ Male Structure & Function Key High School Exam Distinction
Primary Gonads Ovaries (2): Pelvic cavity; cyclic oogenesis (1 ovum/month from puberty to menopause). Testes (2): External scrotum (~35°C); continuous spermatogenesis (millions/day from puberty). Spermatogenesis requires ~2°C cooler than 37°C core temperature; oogenesis starts before birth!
Sex Hormones Estrogen (follicles) & Progesterone (corpus luteum). Testosterone (interstitial Leydig cells between seminiferous tubules). Both regulated by anterior pituitary gonadotropins (FSH & LH).
Gamete Cells Ovum (Secondary Oocyte): Large (~120 µm), non-motile, rich yolk/cytoplasm reserves + zona pellucida. Spermatozoon: Tiny (~60 µm), motile flagellum, acrosome enzymes, mitochondrial midpiece. Both are haploid ($n = 23$) produced via meiosis; fuse at fertilization in ampulla to form diploid ($2n = 46$) zygote.
Gamete Transport Ducts Fallopian Tubes (Oviducts): Ciliated epithelium + smooth muscle sweep egg toward uterus. Epididymis → Vas Deferens: Stores/matures sperm, then propels via rapid peristalsis over bladder. Surgical sterilization cuts/clips Fallopian Tubes (Tubal Ligation) vs. Vas Deferens (Vasectomy); hormones continue normally!
Accessory Secretions / Support Endometrium & Cervical Mucus: Vascular lining nourishes implanted embryo; mucus gates sperm entry. Seminal Vesicles, Prostate & Cowper’s: Secrete fructose, alkaline buffers & activating enzymes (Semen). Seminal fluid (~95% of semen volume) supplies ATP fuel (fructose) and neutralizes acidic urethra & vagina.
Urethra & Urinary Relation Separate Tracts: Female urethra (anterior) carries ONLY urine; Vagina (central) is reproductive/birth canal. Shared Urethra: Carries BOTH urine (from bladder) and semen (via ejaculatory ducts), never simultaneously. Classic sagittal diagram trap: in females, anterior-to-posterior order is Bladder/Urethra → Uterus/Vagina → Rectum!
