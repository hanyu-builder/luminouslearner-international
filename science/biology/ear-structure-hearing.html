👂 Ear Structure & Hearing

Acoustic-Mechanical Translation

Sound waves vibrate the Tympanic Membrane, shifting the bony Ossicles (malleus, incus, stapes).

This lever system amplifies the force and pushes fluid through the Cochlea, creating a traveling wave along the basilar membrane.

High Frequencies peak at the stiff base.
Low Frequencies peak at the floppy apex.
