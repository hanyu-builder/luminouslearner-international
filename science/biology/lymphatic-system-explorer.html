Starling Forces

Fluid exchange in tissues is governed by two opposing forces:
1. Hydrostatic Pressure (HP): Blood pressure pushing fluid OUT of capillaries.
2. Osmotic Pressure (OP): Blood proteins (albumin) pulling fluid IN.
The lymphatic system drains the excess fluid to prevent swelling (Edema).
