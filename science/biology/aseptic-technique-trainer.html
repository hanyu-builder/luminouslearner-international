Aseptic Technique

Aseptic technique is a strict set of procedures used to prevent the contamination of cultures, lab workers, and the environment. Follow the protocol exactly to successfully streak the plate.

Protocol Checklist

1. Ignite Bunsen Burner
Click the burner to create a sterile updraft zone.
2. Flame Inoculating Loop
Drag loop into flame until wire glows red hot.
3. Cool the Loop
Wait 3 seconds. Do not touch anything while hot!
4. Obtain Culture
Click tube cap near flame to open. Dip loop inside.
5. Streak the Plate
Drag inoculated loop over the agar plate to streak.
6. Final Sterilization
Flame the loop once more to kill residual bacteria.
Follow the checklist. Click the Bunsen Burner to start.
