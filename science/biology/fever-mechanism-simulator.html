Exam Concepts: Why do we get fevers?

The Myth: Fever is a symptom of the virus "damaging" or "boiling" your body.

Biological Reality: Fever is a deliberate defense mechanism. Viruses evolved to have an Optimal Temperature of exactly 37°C. By raising the temperature, the body mathematically shifts the odds:

  • Heat causes slight conformational changes (denaturation) in viral surface proteins. If the shape changes, it can no longer bind to host cell receptors to replicate.
  • Immune cells (like Macrophages) actually have a higher optimal temperature. The heat increases their kinetic energy, supercharging their movement and rate of Phagocytosis (engulfing the virus).
