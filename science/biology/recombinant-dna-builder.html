Recombinant DNA

Genetic Engineering Lab

To create Recombinant DNA, scientists cut a bacterial plasmid and a target human gene using the same restriction enzyme, generating complementary "sticky ends". The hybrid DNA is then inserted back into a bacteria to act as a protein factory!

1

Cleave Plasmid Vector

Drag EcoRI to the blue plasmid. It scans for the palindrome GAATTC to slice.

2

Isolate Target Gene

Drag EcoRI to the human DNA. It slices out the insulin gene, leaving complementary sticky ends.

3

Annealing

Drag the isolated Human Gene into the open Plasmid gap. The sticky ends form temporary hydrogen bonds.

4

Ligation

Click the button below to add DNA Ligase, permanently sealing the sugar-phosphate backbone.

5

Transformation

Click the button below to initiate Heat Shock, forcing the bacterium to take up the recombinant plasmid!
