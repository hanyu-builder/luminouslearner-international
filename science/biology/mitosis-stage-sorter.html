Phase Dictionary

  • Prophase: Chromatin condenses into chromosomes. Nucleus dissolves.
  • Metaphase: Chromosomes align at the equator.
  • Anaphase: Sister chromatids are pulled apart.
  • Telophase: Cleavage furrow forms; nuclei rebuild.
