Dentition Exam Reference: Human Stages & Mammalian Dietary Adaptations

Core syllabus comparison for IGCSE, IB, HKDSE, A-Level & AP Biology.

1. Human Diphyodont Dentition: Milk (Deciduous) vs. Permanent Teeth

Feature Milk / Deciduous Dentition (Child) Permanent Dentition (Adult)
Quadrant Dental Formula i 2/2, c 1/1, pm 0/0, m 2/2 = 20 total I 2/2, C 1/1, PM 2/2, M 3/3 = 32 total
Premolars & Wisdom Teeth 0 Premolars and no 3rd molars; baby molars are replaced by adult premolars! 8 Premolars (2 per quadrant) + 12 Molars (including 3rd wisdom molars).
Enamel & Roots Thinner enamel, relatively larger pulp chambers; flared roots resorb before shedding (~6–12 yrs). Thicker heavily mineralized enamel; deep roots; cannot be naturally replaced if lost.

2. Comparative Mammalian Dentition & Diet Adaptations

Tooth / Jaw Feature Omnivore (Human) Herbivore (Sheep / Rabbit) Carnivore (Dog / Lion)
Incisors Chisel-shaped for biting/cutting fruits, vegetables & meat. Crop grass against upper horny pad (sheep) or grow continuously (rodents/rabbits). Small, pointed for nibbling meat cleanly off bones.
Canines & Diastema Modest pointed cuspids flush with arch; no diastema gap. Absent or incisor-like, leaving a wide toothless gap (Diastema) for tongue manipulation! Long, curved dagger-like canines for seizing, stabbing & tearing prey.
Premolars & Molars Blunt rounded cusps (bunodont) for general crushing & grinding. Flat, broad with hard transverse enamel ridges; side-to-side jaw grinding ruptures cellulose walls. Sharp jagged Carnassial teeth (upper PM4 + lower M1) slide past like scissor blades to slice flesh & crack bone; vertical hinge jaw.
