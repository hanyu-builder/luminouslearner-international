📐 Physics Engine Model

This simulation employs a heuristic 2D implementation of the Drude model of electrical conduction to visualize macroscopic solid-state principles:

  • Carrier Density (n): Changing the metal valency spawns more delocalized electrons. The dashboard's Relative Current scales linearly with this density (I ∝ n · vd).
  • Temperature & Resistance: As temperature rises, lattice ions vibrate more (phonons). The simulation calculates a scattering factor (R ∝ 1 + T × α) that physically impedes electron drift.
  • Absolute Zero (0 K): At 0 K, lattice vibrations cease entirely. Without phonon scattering, simulated electrons achieve maximum drift velocity through the perfectly still lattice.
  • Malleability: When mechanical force is applied, lattice layers shear and slide. Because the electrons are fluid, they adapt to the new lattice positions, maintaining the electrostatic bonds and preventing the metal from shattering.
