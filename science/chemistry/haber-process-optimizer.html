Thermodynamic Principles

Le Chatelier's Principle (Yield)

• Exothermic: High temp pushes equilibrium to the left, lowering ammonia yield.

• Volume: 4 moles gas → 2 moles gas. High pressure pushes equilibrium to the right, increasing yield.

Arrhenius Kinetics & Catalyst

• Low temperatures make the reaction uselessly slow. A catalyst and moderate heat are required for an acceptable rate of reaction.

Note: The iron catalyst speeds up the forward and reverse reactions equally. It does not change the final equilibrium yield; it only allows the plant to reach that yield much faster.

The Economic Compromise

"We need it fast enough to be profitable, but cool enough to actually produce ammonia." Optimal operating conditions peak where Yield and Rate intersect favorably. Very high pressures are penalized due to extreme equipment costs and safety risks.

The Industrial Secret: Recycling

Because a single pass only yields ~15%, industrial plants cool the mixture to condense and remove liquid ammonia, then recycle unreacted N₂ and H₂ back into the reactor to achieve a ~98% total conversion rate.
