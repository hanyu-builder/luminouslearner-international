Phase Characteristics

Crystalline Solid
  • High Positional Order: Molecules are locked in a rigid 3D crystal lattice.
  • High Orientational Order: All molecules point in the exact same direction.
Smectic Liquid Crystal
  • Partial Positional Order: Molecules arrange themselves in distinct 1D layers, but slide around freely within the layer.
  • High Orientational Order: Molecules remain aligned with the director axis.
Nematic Liquid Crystal
  • No Positional Order: Molecules move randomly in 3D space like a true liquid.
  • High Orientational Order: Molecules generally align parallel to a common axis (the director). This makes them responsive to electric fields (LCDs).
Isotropic Liquid
  • No Positional Order: Completely random distribution.
  • No Orientational Order: Molecules tumble and point in random directions. The substance is optically clear and behaves like water.
